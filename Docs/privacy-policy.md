# Anew Link privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Anew Link is a free, open-source, non-profit app that connects your iPhone to a Sony Wena 3 band. It is an unofficial app, not made by or affiliated with Sony.

**In short: Anew Link has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no servers of its own. Your data stays on your iPhone and your band, except for the one weather request described below.**

## What the app uses, and why

| Data | What the app does with it | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| **Bluetooth** | Connects to your band to read its battery and firmware, and to send it the time, weather, song titles and calendar events. | Your band only. |
| **Approximate location** | Gets the weather forecast for your area. The position is rounded to about 1 km before it's used. | Sent, rounded, to [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com) to fetch the forecast. The last rounded position is also kept on your iPhone so weather can be updated in the background. Open-Meteo is a free weather service; see its own terms and privacy policy. |
| **Apple Health** (write only) | Saves steps and heart rate recorded by your band. The app never reads your Health data. | Apple Health on your iPhone. |
| **Calendar** (read only) | Shows your next 7 days of events on your band, if you switch this on. The app never changes your calendar. | Your band only. |
| **Apple Music** | Shows the song that's playing on your band's music screen, if you switch this on. | Your band only. |
| **Notifications** | Rings your phone when your band asks to find it, if you switch this on. | Your iPhone only. |
| **Band log** | A diagnostic list of connections and requests from the band (times and request types, never song titles, event names or your band's serial number). | Your iPhone only. You can share it yourself from the debug screen. |

Each of these is optional. The app asks for permission the first time a feature needs it, and you can turn any permission off in the iPhone's Settings.

## What the app does not do

- It does not collect, sell or share personal data.
- It has no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking.
- It does not send anything to Sony or to the app's developer.
- It does not read your Health data, change your calendar, or access your contacts or messages.

## Children

The app is not aimed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published in the app's source repository with a new date. The app is open source, so anyone can check what it does. Questions: please open an issue in the app's GitHub repository.
